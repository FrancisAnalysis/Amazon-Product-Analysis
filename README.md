# Amazon Product Dataset Analysis 📊

## Project Overview

This project focuses on cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing an Amazon product dataset using **Microsoft Excel**. The goal was to explore product pricing, discount percentages, customer ratings, rating counts, and category representation to identify patterns and generate meaningful insights.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), data validation, PivotTables, data visualization, and dashboard development.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **1,465 product records** and includes information such as:

- Product ID and product name
- Product category
- Actual price and discounted price
- Discount percentage
- Product rating and rating count
- Product descriptions and customer reviews

## Tools and Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and validation
- Excel formulas and helper columns
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- KPI development
- Dashboard design and data visualization

## Project Process

### 1. Data Cleaning and Validation
- Examined the dataset for missing values and data inconsistencies.
- Investigated repeated product IDs and potential duplicate records.
- Identified inconsistencies between actual prices, discounted prices, and listed discount percentages.
- Created helper columns to investigate price corrections and calculate discounts from price values.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis
Analyzed the dataset to explore:

- Product category representation
- Average product ratings
- Discount percentage distribution
- Average ratings across discount bands
- Average customer rating counts across discount bands
- Highly rated products with substantial rating counts
- Pricing differences across selected categories

### 3. Dashboard Development
Created an Excel dashboard featuring KPI metrics and charts to communicate the findings clearly. The dashboard brings together product record counts, unique product IDs, average ratings, discount statistics, category comparisons, and customer feedback patterns.

## Key Insights

- **Category representation:** USB cables were the most represented category in the category results analyzed, with 233 records.
- **Product ratings:** The rating distribution showed that most records fell within the 4–5 rating range.
- **Discounts and ratings:** Average ratings across discount bands varied only slightly, from 4.03 to 4.13, with no strong visible pattern.
- **Customer feedback:** The 50–59% discount band had the highest average recorded rating count among the discount bands analyzed.
- **Highly rated products:** The Swiffer Instant Electric Water Heater Faucet Tap and Instant Pot Vortex 2QT Air Fryer both had average ratings of 4.8 in the filtered product analysis.
- **Data quality:** The project highlighted inconsistencies in listed discount percentages and price-derived discounts, demonstrating the importance of validating data before drawing conclusions.

## Data Limitations

- Repeated product IDs may affect category counts and rating-count summaries.
- Rating counts represent recorded ratings, not product sales.
- Price corrections based on decimal placement are assumptions and should be verified where possible.
- Some PivotTable totals and overall rating figures required further reconciliation before final reporting.

These limitations were documented to support responsible interpretation of the results.
